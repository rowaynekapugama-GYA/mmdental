What NIB First Choice means for you

NIB has approved M&M Dental Care as a First Choice provider. In plain terms, NIB members get more back from their extras cover here than at a non-preferred dentist.

  • Higher rebates, less out of pocketFirst Choice providers agree to capped fees, so your NIB rebate covers more of the bill.
  • No gap on selected services*Depending on your level of cover, routine care such as a check-up and clean can have nothing to pay on the day.
  • Claim on the spot with HICAPSSwipe your NIB card at reception and the rebate is processed instantly.

Common questions

I'm with NIB. What will I pay?

As an NIB First Choice provider, we charge capped fees for NIB members and process your rebate instantly through HICAPS. On many routine services, such as a check-up and clean, members with suitable extras cover pay no gap at all. Bring your card and we will confirm your cover before we start.

How do the payment plans work?

We offer interest-free plans through Afterpay and Humm (National Dental Plan). Your treatment cost is split into instalments with no interest charged, and our front desk can set it up during your visit. Plans are available for all services, from cleans to implants and Invisalign.

Are you really open until 10pm?

Yes. We open 9am to 10pm Monday to Friday and 9am to 5pm on weekends and public holidays, so you can see a dentist after work or school without taking time off.

Do you bulk bill children?

Yes. Children aged 2 to 17 who are eligible for the Medicare Child Dental Benefits Schedule receive over $1,000 of dental care over two years with nothing to pay. Call us to confirm your child's eligibility.

What should I do in a dental emergency?

Call 02 8631 1525 as early as you can. We keep same-day emergency appointments open every day of the week, including evenings until 10pm on weekdays.

Do I need to be an existing patient?

No. New patients are welcome and can book online any time or call us during opening hours.
