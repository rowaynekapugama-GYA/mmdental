What happens next

Three quick things so your first visit runs smoothly.

1

We confirm your time

You will get a confirmation by SMS or email from the practice. If anything needs adjusting, reply or give us a call and we will sort it.

2

Bring your cards

Bring your health fund card so we can claim on the spot through HICAPS, plus your Medicare card if your child is eligible for CDBS bulk billing.

3

Arrive a few minutes early

There is free on-site parking with no time limits at 861 Richmond Road. New patients can complete a short form at reception on arrival.

First Choice Preferred Provider

With NIB? You are in the right place.

As a First Choice provider we charge NIB members capped fees and process your rebate instantly, so on many routine visits there is nothing to pay on the day. Not with NIB? Every major fund is accepted and interest-free plans are available through Afterpay and Humm.

  • Higher on-the-spot rebates for NIB extras cover
  • No gap on selected services, subject to your level of cover
  • Flexible, interest-free payment plans on any treatment
